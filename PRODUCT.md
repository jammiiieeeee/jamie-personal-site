# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

No single transactional audience. The job is existential: leave a trace. The site is an owned, permanent digital footprint — the one place where the full, curated picture of the person exists beyond any platform profile.

## Product Purpose

A polished professional home base. It establishes who you are, what you do, and why you're skilled at it. The site carries resume/CV content as its core substance — work history, skills, achievements — presented as a coherent whole rather than a checklist.

## Positioning

Unlike LinkedIn or GitHub — rented format, rented space, rented identity — this is an owned space with complete creative control. It's the one URL that survives platform churn and presents you on your own terms.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, email signatures, or direct search. They scan for credibility signals, work history, and personality. The site must load fast and read well on any device.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site (no framework, no build step, no database) — portable, fast, easy to host anywhere
- Resume/CV content is partly ready
- No existing brand assets confirmed
- No existing domain confirmed

## Product Principles

1. **Owned presence.** This is your space, not a platform's. The design reflects you, not a template.
2. **Substance over noise.** Content is the point. The site presents it clearly, without distraction.
3. **Simple and durable.** Static means fast, portable, and won't rot. Every decision should honor that.
4. **A trace that lasts.** The site should feel intentional — worth leaving behind.
